Studio Walkthrough & Shortcuts

Interactive Canvas Controls

  • Drag Main Title: Click and drag the cyan Title directly on canvas to reposition.
  • Drag Sub-Title: Click and drag the yellow Sub-title directly on canvas to reposition independently.
  • Visual Crop Mode: Click "Visual Crop" in the top bar to display interactive crop handles. Drag corner/edge handles to resize, drag inside to pan, and scroll mouse wheel over canvas to zoom!
  • Click Canvas: Click anywhere on the video preview canvas to toggle Play / Pause!
  • Spacebar: Toggle Play / Pause instantly.

Vocal Muting & Subtitle Import

  • Mute Singer Voice: Click "Mute Singer Voice (Music Only)" to mute the singer and keep the backing track. The system also detects lead vocals automatically!
  • Auto-Crop Subtitles: Changing the audio trim automatically crops the subtitles and lyrics to match the cut.
  • YouTube Transcript: Open any YouTube video with captions, open transcript, copy lines, and paste into the Import modal.
