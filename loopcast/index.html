EasyLoop Cast — Help Guide & Features

Step-by-step broadcast workflow & live tools overview

STEP 1 Create Your Playlist & Add Videos or Folders
Go to the Queue / Playlists tab in the navigation grid:
  • Add Local Files: Pick video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov, .mkv) directly from your computer.
  • Add Folder: Import an entire directory of videos in one click.
  • Add YouTube URL: Paste direct YouTube links to stream cloud videos into the playlist.
  • Persistent Storage: Files are stored in browser IndexedDB — they persist even after page reloads!
  • Reorder & Loop: Drag and drop videos to set order. Turn on Loop Playlist for continuous 24/7 playback.
STEP 2 Choose Frame Aspect Ratio & Fit Mode
Go to the Frame tab:
  • Aspect Ratio: Choose 9:16 Vertical for YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, TikTok & Instagram Live. Or choose 16:9 Landscape for standard YouTube & Facebook broadcasts. 1:1, 4:5, and 21:9 are also available.
  • Fit Mode:
    • Fill (Zoom & Crop): 100% Edge-to-edge frame. Fills the screen with zero black bars.
    • Contain: Shows the full uncropped video centered on a clean background.
    • Letterbox: Standard centered display with letterbox bars.
  • Zoom & Pan: Use sliders to zoom in/out or shift X/Y position to center important visual content.
STEP 3 Customize FX, Audio/BGM, Overlays & Logo Cover
Enhance your live broadcast with professional real-time tools:
  • FX & Filters (FX Tab): Adjust Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Blur, Grayscale, Sepia, or toggle Mirror Mode in real time.
  • Audio & BGM (Audio Tab): Balance Master audio volume, mute/unmute, or upload custom Background Music (BGM) with auto-ducking (automatically softens BGM when video dialogue plays).
  • Channel Logo & Overlays (Logo & Overlay Tabs): Upload custom PNG watermarks, sponsor logos, or ticker text. Position them in any corner with custom opacity and scale.
  • Logo Cover / Masking (Masking Tab): Cover up unwanted source watermarks, timestamps, or broadcaster badges using a blur patch or color patch.
  • Clean Pop-Out / Maximize: Pop out a clean, borderless stage window for secondary monitors or OBS integration.
STEP 4 Add Streaming URLs, Stream Keys & Bridge Settings
Go to the Go Live tab:
  • Platform Stream Keys:
    • YouTube Live: Paste your stream key from YouTube Studio.
    • Facebook Live: Paste your stream key from Facebook Live Producer.
    • Instagram / TikTok / Custom: Paste stream URL and stream key.
  • Multi-Stream (Simulcast): Toggle "Simulcast Multi-Stream" ON to broadcast to YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram all at the exact same time!
  • Bridge Quality & Bitrate: Select 1080p30 Full HD (recommended bitrate: 4500k-5000k for rock-solid stability without buffering).
STEP 5 Hit 'Start Broadcast' & '🔴 GO LIVE'!
  • Click Start Broadcast in the Live Deck to start playing the video playlist on the stage.
  • Click 🔴 GO LIVE in the RTMP panel.
  • The bridge connects instantly via WebSocket to FFmpeg. Your stream status will switch to 🔴 LIVE with real-time on-air timer, live bitrate, and total megabytes streamed!
100% Client-Side Privacy — No Registration, No Login, No External Database
EasyLoop Cast requires NO account, NO email, NO password, and NO cloud login. Everything operates securely and directly inside your local browser.
HOW IT WORKS How Data is Stored Locally on Your Device
Your browser uses two built-in browser storage mechanisms:
  • Browser IndexedDB (Video File Storage): When you pick local video files or folders, the files are stored inside your browser's dedicated IndexedDB database directly on your computer's hard drive. They never leave your device.
  • LocalStorage (Settings & Stream Keys): Playlists, stream keys, aspect ratio preferences, visual FX, and audio settings are preserved in LocalStorage.
  • Instant Persistence: Because data is stored in the browser, you can refresh the page, close the tab, or reopen it anytime — your playlists and settings remain ready to go.
IMPORTANT WARNING What Happens if You Clear Browser History & Storage?
Because all media and settings reside exclusively in your local browser storage:
  • Clearing "Cookies and Other Site Data": If you clean your browser history, cache, or site data ("Clear browsing data"), your browser will erase the local IndexedDB database.
  • Re-adding Required: If storage is wiped by your browser, your playlists and local files will be removed, and you will need to re-add your videos and re-paste your stream keys.
  • Private / Incognito Browsing: If you use an Incognito / Private Window, browsers automatically discard all IndexedDB and LocalStorage as soon as the window is closed. Always use a regular browser window for persistent storage!
BACKUP & RESTORE Protect Your Setup With 1-Click Backup
To prevent accidental data loss:
  • Click the Settings (Sliders) icon in the top header.
  • Click Download Backup (JSON) to save your entire studio setup, playlists, and stream keys to a small backup file on your computer.
  • If you ever clean your browser or switch computers, simply click Choose File under Settings to restore your entire studio in 1 second!
